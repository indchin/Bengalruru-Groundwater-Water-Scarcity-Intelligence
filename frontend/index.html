ⓘ Data & AI disclaimer: This prototype is built from REAL published figures — Karnataka Minor Irrigation Dept taluk-level groundwater change, CGWB depth bulletins, the 2023 statewide drought declaration, IMD rainfall records, BWSSB Cauvery Stage I–V capacity, CGWB's 2023 rural water-quality assessment, an IISc lake-area study, and named locality reports (all cited with dates and links in Data Sources). No public feed gives a verified daily reading for all 28 localities shown, so each area's year-by-year trajectory and quality indicators are REAL‑CALIBRATED — generated once and blended to match the real taluk/zone anchor for that area — rather than a live per-street measurement. All forecasts, crisis scores and "what‑if" results remain model estimates for exploration — not official government warnings or guaranteed outcomes.

Data Provenance

This prototype is built so each of these could be swapped for a live feed behind a small internal API layer (/data/groundwater, /data/rainfall, /data/water-sources, /data/water-quality, /data/predictions) without changing the UI. Every card below marked REAL is an exact or directly-cited published figure with a date and outlet; the model card at the end is the one part of this app that is explicitly a computed estimate rather than a measurement. Where a real figure exists only at taluk, zone or city level (not for all 28 individual localities), this app blends each locality's modeled trajectory to match that real anchor — flagged REAL‑CALIBRATED throughout the Map, List and Area panels — rather than presenting an unverified number as if it were a direct reading.
